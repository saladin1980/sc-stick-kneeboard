Stick kneeboard

One card per stick, with every Star Citizen mode on each button. Made from your Joystick Diagrams exports.

Drop your exports here

All the SVG files Joystick Diagrams wrote, every mode at once. PNGs can come along too; they're skipped.

Your files stay in this browser tab. The page makes no network requests and uploads nothing.

Three steps

  1. In Joystick Diagrams, export your Star Citizen profile. It writes one SVG per stick for each mode.
  2. Drop all of those SVG files on the bay, or choose them.
  3. Download a card for each stick: SVG stays sharp when zoomed, PNG is ready for Discord.

Button names are mapped for the VKB Gladiator templates. Other sticks still merge; their buttons show as slot positions.

Built on Joystick Diagrams exports (github.com/Rexeh/joystick-diagrams, GPL-2.0). Not affiliated with Cloud Imperium Games.
