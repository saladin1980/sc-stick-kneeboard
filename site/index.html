Three steps

  1. In Star Citizen, open Options, Keybindings, and export your controls. The game writes an XML file.
  2. Drop that file on the bay, or choose it.
  3. Pick each stick's grip, then download its card. PNG is ready for Discord; SVG stays sharp when zoomed.

Pictures are VKB Gladiator templates (SCG and SCG Omni-Throttle, left and right). Other sticks get a full list instead of a picture.

Stick templates: VKB Gladiator fillable templates by VKB (vkbgaming.com), design by UntoldForce, free to use per VKB. Control names and defaults: Star Citizen's own game data. An unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Cloud Imperium Games or VKB.
